# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or location.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific in feedback.
- Assume good intent while addressing the impact of words and actions.
- Welcome differing technical and professional perspectives.
- Focus discussion on learners, evidence, and the work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish private information or confidential customer material.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove or edit contributions that violate them. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the contact method listed in the GitHub repository profile. If no private contact is configured, open a minimal issue asking maintainers for a confidential reporting route; do not post sensitive details publicly.

This policy is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant. It is a project-specific summary and does not reproduce its licensed text.
