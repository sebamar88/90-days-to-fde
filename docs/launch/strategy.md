# Launch strategy

## Goal

Build an initial audience of experienced engineers and gather evidence that the curriculum solves a real learning problem. The launch is a free curriculum preview, not a commercial-product launch.

## Audience hypothesis

Senior engineers exploring FDE or applied AI roles need a practical way to demonstrate customer discovery, production judgment, and AI evaluation together. Test this hypothesis before investing in a platform, paid content, or certification.

## Sequence

1. **Prepare:** publish the README, curriculum map, first 15 lessons, contribution guide, and landing page. Ask a small group of experienced engineers to review the path.
2. **Learn:** conduct short opt-in conversations; ask about current learning attempts, missing skills, and evidence employers value. Keep notes private and report only consented, aggregated themes.
3. **Preview:** share a clear invitation to try Days 1–3 and return feedback. Avoid claims about jobs or outcomes not supported by evidence.
4. **Iterate:** fix the largest comprehension and assessment problems; publish decisions and open issues.
5. **Expand:** complete Phase 2 only after the first learning path is understandable and contributors can run the checks.

## Channels

Start with the repository, existing professional networks, and relevant engineering communities where sharing educational resources is welcome. Follow each community's posting rules; do not mass-message or spam. A static page links directly to the open curriculum.

## Demand validation

Track only lightweight, privacy-conscious signals initially: voluntary lesson feedback, issue discussions, contribution quality, and whether learners return with completed artifacts. Do not treat stars/views as proof of learning or commercial demand. If future payment tests are considered, ask learners directly about unmet needs and willingness to pay before building premium products.

## Feedback prompts

- What did you expect this lesson to help you do?
- What part was unclear or felt unlike real customer work?
- Which artifact would you be comfortable showing in a portfolio, and why?
- What blocked you from continuing?
- What would you choose to learn next?

## Launch readiness checklist

- [ ] Audience and non-guaranteed outcomes are clear.
- [ ] Days 1–15 pass structural validation and human editorial review.
- [ ] Licensing, third-party attribution, and contribution paths are visible.
- [ ] No paid product, required account, tracker, or secret is needed to start.
- [ ] Maintainers can respond to feedback and correct issues.
