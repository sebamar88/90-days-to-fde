- **Code and scripts:** MIT, as stated in `LICENSE`.
- **Original curriculum and other educational prose:** CC BY-SA 4.0, as stated in `LICENSE-CONTENT.md`. Reusers must provide attribution, link to the license, indicate changes, and share adaptations under the same or a compatible license. Commercial reuse is permitted; this is intentional and consistent with the project's open-education goals.
- **Brand assets and name:** copyright remains with their stated creators; the content license does not grant trademark rights. See `docs/brand/identity.md`.

These notices express project policy; contributors should consult qualified counsel for legal advice about a specific reuse or contribution.

## Contributions

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that you have the right to submit it and agree that, unless explicitly marked otherwise and accepted by maintainers, your original code contribution is offered under MIT and original educational content under CC BY-SA 4.0. No separate copyright assignment is requested. Do not contribute confidential, personal, or improperly copied content.

## Third-party material

Prefer original explanations and links to authoritative sources over copied passages, screenshots, datasets, or diagrams. Before including third-party material:

1. Record the title, creator, source URL, license/terms, and changes in the file or `docs/third-party-attributions.md`.
2. Confirm that the license permits the intended redistribution and adaptation. A public webpage is not automatically reusable.
3. Preserve required notices and distinguish the material from project-authored content.
4. If terms are unclear or incompatible, link to the source instead of copying it.

Do not include real customer data, proprietary prompts, access credentials, or datasets with unclear rights. Lessons should use synthetic examples.
