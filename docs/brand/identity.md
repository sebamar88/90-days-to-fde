# Brand identity

## Positioning

**90 Days to FDE** is a field guide for experienced engineers learning to deliver software where customer context, operational reality, and applied AI meet.

## Voice

- Direct, technically credible, and useful.
- Curious about the customer's work; skeptical of solution-first claims.
- Clear about evidence, uncertainty, risk, and trade-offs.
- Welcoming to international contributors; avoid idioms and unexplained acronyms.

## Visual direction

- **Motif:** a route/compass path ending at a terminal cursor, suggesting discovery and delivery.
- **Palette:** deep navy `#102338`, warm white `#F7F5EF`, electric teal `#22C7B8`, amber `#F4B942` for secondary emphasis.
- **Typography:** system sans-serif for accessible body text; monospace only for technical accents.
- **Contrast:** use navy on warm white for body text; teal/amber are accents, not small-text backgrounds.
- **Mark:** use the simple SVG route mark in `assets/brand/mark.svg`; pair it with the name. Keep the name readable at small sizes.

## Usage

Do not imply affiliation with model vendors or employers. Do not use a provider logo as a substitute for the project mark. The project name and mark are identity assets; permission to reuse curriculum under CC BY does not grant endorsement or trademark rights. Accessibility and readability take priority over decorative effects.
