# Product vision

## Vision

Make the transition from senior software engineer to customer-facing AI systems builder concrete, rigorous, and accessible worldwide.

## Product promise

Learners practice the full delivery loop: understand a real business workflow, agree on a measurable problem, build a useful system, evaluate and secure it, deploy responsibly, and explain trade-offs to technical and non-technical stakeholders.

## Principles

- **Production first:** reliability, security, privacy, maintainability, and operating cost are part of the design from the start.
- **Learn by building:** every unit produces evidence or a working artifact.
- **Business context matters:** technical choices connect to a user, workflow, constraint, and outcome.
- **Provider agnostic:** teach stable concepts and interfaces; treat providers as interchangeable implementations.
- **Free first:** no paid API, subscription, or hosted platform is required for core learning.
- **Open and international:** English is canonical; contributions and later translations are welcome.
- **Honest outcomes:** teach capability and evidence, not guaranteed employment or certification.

## Success hypotheses to validate

1. Experienced engineers want a structured path that combines customer discovery with applied AI delivery.
2. A portfolio of realistic artifacts is more useful than a sequence of disconnected demos.
3. Free, provider-neutral material with optional local-model paths lowers participation barriers.

Validate through interviews, issue discussions, starts/completions, and learner feedback. Do not interpret stars or page views alone as proof of learning or willingness to pay.

## Non-goals for Phase 1

No commercial product, proprietary lab, paid certificate, hosted learner tracking, job placement claim, or single-provider course requirement.
