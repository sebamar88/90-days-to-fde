# Curriculum roadmap

The curriculum spans 90 guided learning days across six phases. Days 1–15 are ready; later days are currently roadmap topics. The sequence is flexible and does not guarantee employment or a fixed daily workload.

| Phase | Days | Focus | Status |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 | 1–15 | Foundations & Business Discovery | Ready |
| 2 | 16–30 | Production AI Engineering | Planned |
| 3 | 31–45 | Agentic Systems & Enterprise Integration | Planned |
| 4 | 46–60 | Reliability, Evaluation & Security | Planned |
| 5 | 61–75 | Enterprise Deployment & Delivery | Planned |
| 6 | 76–90 | Capstone & Career Readiness | Planned |

See the [detailed day-by-day curriculum map](curriculum/README.md), the [Phase 1 overview](curriculum/phase-1-foundations/README.md), and the [Phase 1 lessons](curriculum/phase-1-foundations/).

## Roadmap principles

- Start from business discovery and keep AI as a hypothesis to test.
- Build cumulative artifacts toward a customer-ready capstone.
- Include practical work and observable assessments in every completed lesson.
- Keep the core path provider-agnostic and free-first.
- Use synthetic scenarios and cite authoritative, current sources for technical claims.
