# Curriculum roadmap

The curriculum spans 90 suggested learning days across six phases. All days have authored lesson drafts and assessments, but the lessons remain public preview, unreviewed, and not validated with learners. This roadmap tracks content inventory, not mastery, deployment readiness, or demand validation. The sequence does not guarantee employment or a fixed daily workload.

| Phase | Days | Focus | Status |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 | 1–15 | Foundations & Business Discovery | Drafts authored; Day 1 expanded; review and learner validation pending |
| 2 | 16–30 | Production AI Engineering | Drafts and local reference starter; review and learner validation pending |
| 3 | 31–45 | Agentic Systems & Enterprise Integration | Drafts authored; review and learner validation pending |
| 4 | 46–60 | Reliability, Evaluation & Security | Drafts authored; review and learner validation pending |
| 5 | 61–75 | Enterprise Deployment & Delivery | Drafts authored; review and learner validation pending |

## Roadmap principles

- Start from business discovery and keep AI as a hypothesis to test.
- Build cumulative artifacts toward a capstone that can be demonstrated and reviewed; customer readiness must be established separately in a real context.
- Include practical work and observable assessments in every completed lesson.
- Keep the core path provider-agnostic and free-first.
- Use synthetic scenarios and cite authoritative, current sources for technical claims.
