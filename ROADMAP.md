# Curriculum roadmap

The curriculum spans 90 guided learning days across six phases. All 90 days now have lesson pages and assessments; learner feedback and demand validation remain ongoing. The sequence is flexible and does not guarantee employment or a fixed daily workload.

| Phase | Days | Focus | Status |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 | 1–15 | Foundations & Business Discovery | Complete; learner feedback ongoing |
| 2 | 16–30 | Production AI Engineering | Lessons and local reference starter ready |
| 3 | 31–45 | Agentic Systems & Enterprise Integration | Lessons ready |
| 4 | 46–60 | Reliability, Evaluation & Security | Lessons ready |
| 5 | 61–75 | Enterprise Deployment & Delivery | Lessons ready |
| 6 | 76–90 | Capstone & Career Readiness | Lessons ready |

See the [detailed day-by-day curriculum map](curriculum/README.md), [Phase 1](curriculum/phase-1-foundations/README.md), [Phase 2](curriculum/phase-2-production-ai/README.md), [Phase 3](curriculum/phase-3-agentic-systems/README.md), [Phase 4](curriculum/phase-4-reliability-security/README.md), [Phase 5](curriculum/phase-5-enterprise-delivery/README.md), and [Phase 6](curriculum/phase-6-capstone-career/README.md).

## Roadmap principles

- Start from business discovery and keep AI as a hypothesis to test.
- Build cumulative artifacts toward a customer-ready capstone.
- Include practical work and observable assessments in every completed lesson.
- Keep the core path provider-agnostic and free-first.
- Use synthetic scenarios and cite authoritative, current sources for technical claims.
