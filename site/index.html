Public curriculum preview: 90 lesson drafts are authored; independent technical/editorial review and real learner validation are still pending. Meridian Facilities and its evidence are fictional and synthetic. This is a suggested sequence, not a job guarantee or fixed-hours bootcamp. No paid model or platform is required to start.

Production work is more than a demo.

Practice the whole delivery loop: discover the workflow, define success, design around enterprise constraints, build and evaluate, then deploy and hand off responsibly.

01 / DISCOVER

Understand the work

Interview stakeholders, map workflows, and separate evidence from assumptions.

02 / BUILD

Make it useful

Develop bounded AI systems with baselines, human review, and provider-neutral interfaces.

03 / PROVE

Earn production trust

Evaluate failure cases, manage security and reliability, and explain trade-offs.

04 / DELIVER

Plan for adoption

Practice deployment, operations, change, handoff, and portfolio evidence in a synthetic scenario.

Reference labs and automated tests exist across all six phases. They test the reference implementations and curriculum structure; they do not automatically grade every individual lesson exercise.

Start with the customer, not the model.

The first 15 lessons use a fictional facilities workflow to practice discovery and decide whether AI is warranted before writing code. Simulated work is not customer validation.
