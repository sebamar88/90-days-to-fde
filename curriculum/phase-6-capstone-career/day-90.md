# Day 90 — Retrospective and continued learning plan

## Why this matters

Finishing a learning path is a checkpoint, not proof of mastery. A useful retrospective identifies what changed, what remains unknown, and how to keep practicing with feedback.

## Scenario

The learner reviews all 90 days with a peer acting as a technical reviewer. The peer has not seen the work and should challenge unsupported claims.

## Outcomes

- Evaluate the original goals against completed artifacts and assessments.
- Identify strengths, unresolved gaps, and assumptions that did not hold.
- Plan continued practice with measurable checkpoints.

## Exercise

Revisit the Day 1 goals and Day 76 evidence register. Mark each as demonstrated, partially demonstrated, or not evidenced. Review assessment feedback, tests, and demo observations. Select three next skills and write a four-week plan with practice artifact, feedback source, and completion evidence. Ask a peer to challenge one portfolio claim and revise it if needed.

## Deliverable

Retrospective, updated role-evidence map, and four-week learning plan.

## Assessment rubric

Score 0 (missing), 1 (partial), or 2 (evidenced): artifacts and technical trade-offs; customer discovery; safe tool/action boundaries; evaluation honesty; reliability/security; operational readiness; stakeholder communication; failure-path demonstration; portfolio privacy; specific next steps. Pass at 16/20 with no zero for safety, evaluation honesty, failure-path demonstration, privacy, or next-step plan.

## Reflection

What will you stop claiming, start practicing, and keep doing after this curriculum?
