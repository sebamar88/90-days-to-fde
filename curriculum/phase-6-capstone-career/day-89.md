# Day 89 — Build a role-specific evidence map

## Why this matters

Role titles vary across companies. Mapping demonstrated skills to artifacts helps a candidate identify genuine strengths and gaps without treating one curriculum as a credential.

## Scenario

The learner is comparing an FDE role and an Applied AI Engineer role. The example job descriptions are public or fictional and should not be treated as a guarantee of hiring criteria.

## Outcomes

- Extract responsibilities and evidence expectations from a target role.
- Map each claim to a specific artifact or demo.
- Identify gaps that need future practice rather than inflated wording.

## Exercise

Choose one current job description or a fictional role brief. Build a matrix of responsibility, evidence, confidence, and next practice. Link to artifacts for discovery, integration, evaluation, security, delivery, and communication. Mark skills only practiced in simulation separately from skills demonstrated in a deployed customer context. Draft three concise resume bullets with accurate scope.

## Deliverable

Role-specific evidence map and a gap-based practice plan.

## Assessment

Pass when every claim links to evidence, simulated and production experience are distinct, and at least one gap is converted into a concrete next step.

## Reflection

Which skill is visible in your artifacts but hardest to communicate in a short interview answer?
